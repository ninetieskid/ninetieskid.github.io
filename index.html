About Moravia Harvest Imports

Moravia Harvest Imports brings the finest wines of Czechia to the Canadian market. We work directly with boutique Moravian wineries to introduce their heritage-rich, small-batch wines to Canadian businesses seeking something truly unique.

Our mission is to highlight the distinctive terroir and centuries-old craftsmanship of Czech vineyards — a region still undiscovered by many, yet unforgettable to those who taste it.

How We Can Work Together

We are currently onboarding select Canadian partners who value authenticity, regional stories, and outstanding quality. If you are a restaurant, wine merchant, or importer looking to enrich your offering, we'd love to connect.

Details of our collaboration process will be available soon. In the meantime, please contact us to learn more or express your interest.
